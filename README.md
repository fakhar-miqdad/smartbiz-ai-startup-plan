# SmartBiz AI: Startup Execution Plan

**Startup plan, financial model and roadmap for small online sellers in Karachi, Pakistan.**

> **Status:** SmartBiz AI is a **proposed** startup. No product, customers, revenue, partnerships, investors or legal approvals exist yet. All forecasts are planning scenarios, not guaranteed results.

---

## What is SmartBiz AI?

SmartBiz AI is a proposed business management tool for small online sellers and home-based businesses in Karachi. It aims to bring orders, customers, inventory, expenses and basic reports into one simple, PKR-priced system, instead of scattered WhatsApp chats, Instagram messages and spreadsheets.

The problems it targets are **hypotheses** that this plan asks the founders to validate through seller interviews before spending significant money.

## What the plan covers

The document follows 12 steps:

1. Executive summary
2. Problem and market analysis (TAM, SAM, SOM; interview questionnaire; validation plan)
3. Solution and product scope (MVP, features, architecture, roadmap)
4. Competitor analysis and positioning (SWOT, 2x2 matrix)
5. Business model and pricing (tiers in PKR, unit economics)
6. Go-to-market and marketing plan (funnel, 90-day plan, budget)
7. Operations and team structure (roles, org chart, RACI)
8. Legal, regulatory, security and risk plan (risk register)
9. Execution timeline and Gantt chart (52 weeks)
10. Financial plan and investment requirements (3 scenarios, 12-month forecast)
11. KPIs and performance dashboard
12. Immediate next actions

Appendices include financial assumptions, Mermaid Gantt code, a glossary and source links.

## Key planning figures (forecasts, not results)

| Scenario | Funding requirement | Paying customers at month 12 | Customers needed to break even |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lean | PKR 1.05 million | 83 | 53 |
| Standard | PKR 3.45 million | 129 | 142 |
| Growth | PKR 13.70 million | 386 | 584 |

Each funding figure includes a 15% contingency. Taxes and founder salaries (Lean and Standard plans) are not included.

## Important notes

- **Evidence labels:** every market figure and cost is marked as verified, calculated, assumption or quotation required.
- **Market sizing:** no verified count of online sellers in Pakistan was found, so the TAM, SAM and SOM are an illustrative scenario built from labelled assumptions.
- **Competitor data:** taken from public websites in October 2026. Prices change, so verify before use.
- **Legal and tax content:** general information only, not advice. Confirm with a qualified Pakistani lawyer and tax adviser.

## Planned technology (prototype)

Python, Streamlit and SQLite for the MVP, with PostgreSQL considered as users grow. AI features are optional and planned for Phase 2. WhatsApp, courier and payment integrations are planned for Phase 3, subject to approval and charges.
